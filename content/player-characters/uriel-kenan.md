### Character

Ancestry: [[elves|Elf]]. Community: Orderborne. Class: Seraph, subclass Divine Wielder.

## History

### Drinmery

In [[drinmery|Drinmery]] the Inquisitor [[gideon|Gideon]] realized Uriel was a religious extremist: loyal to [[lael|Lael]] rather than to Pontiff [[amalthus-cruoris|Amalthus]], his memories wiped, and convinced that the holy purge that began with [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] must be completed by Inquisitors' own hands. Gideon called him a cultist, but the two agreed to work together against the bandits and Father [[ambrose-mourne|Tobias]]. On the road to the bandits' fort, [[malak|Malak's]] herbs showed him OphDreams. He carries Gideon's sword, given to him as false proof that Gideon died at the fort.

At the churchyard gate on the night of Adventus 16 he fought the risen dead beside Gideon. [[rastaban|Ras]] healed him after the battle. He carries the [[the-threnodies|Threnody]] that Malak delivered and the old priest's Gravewarden's Bell. He and [[novak-azimuth|Novak]] have agreed to work together, and [[rosestripe|Rosestripe]] travels with him to learn about the One Above.
