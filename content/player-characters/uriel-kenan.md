### Character

Ancestry: [[elves|Elf]]. Community: Orderborne. Class: Seraph, subclass Divine Wielder.

## History

### Drinmery

In [[drinmery|Drinmery]] the Inquisitor [[gideon|Gideon]] realized Uriel was a religious extremist: loyal to [[lael|Lael]] rather than to Pontiff [[amalthus-cruoris|Amalthus]], his memories wiped, and convinced that the holy purge that began with [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] must be completed by Inquisitors' own hands. Gideon called him a cultist, but the two agreed to work together against the bandits and [[tobias-wren|Father Tobias]]. On the road to the bandits' fort, [[malak|Malak's]] herbs showed him OphDreams. He carries Gideon's sword, given to him as false proof that Gideon died at the fort.
