**Summary**: Player character. A Seaborne [[simiah|Simiah]] Bard, Wordsmith, out of [[armada|Armada]].

---

> [!note] Player character
> Malak is a PC, not an NPC.

## Appearance

A Simiah with long golden fur that falls past his shoulders and is bound into a wrapped tail at his back, a pale gray face, and a long tail of his own. He wears a tricorn hat banded in orange, a loose white shirt under a red vest, and dark wraps at his wrists, and carries a cutlass. His portrait shows him taking tea, seated on a barrel.

## Abilities

### Character

Ancestry: [[simiah|Simiah]]. Community: Seaborne. Class: Bard, subclass Wordsmith.

## History

### Drinmery

[[guilmore-fleming|Count Fleming]] paid him for the delivery with the deed to a decommissioned merchant ship, docked in [[jestha|Jestha]]. On the night of Adventus 12, 366 VR, he woke on the convent step to find [[gideon|Gideon]] digging up an unmarked grave, and later told [[rosestripe|Rosestripe]] about the locket they found there. On the road to the bandits' fort he shared his herbs with [[uriel-kenan|Uriel]]: Ophanim-tainted herbs grown in [[andaluria|Andaluria]], which give OphDreams even to those who have never seen [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]].
