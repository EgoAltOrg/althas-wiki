> [!note] Player character
> Rosestripe is a PC, not an NPC.

## Appearance

Female, with the angelic look common to her kind: a small frame, a childlike face, and white feathered wings.

## Abilities

### Character

Ancestry: [[aetheris|Aetheris]]. Community: Orderborne. Class: Assassin, subclass Poisoners Guild.

## History

Rosestripe was created six years ago.

### Drinmery

On the night of Adventus 16, 366 VR, she fought in the battle in the convent churchyard. With a wolf spirit, she broke through the holy shield of the convent's priest, unmasked that night as Ambrose Mourne, and brought him down. She protested when the Inquisitor [[gideon|Gideon]] took his head, and stole the locket from him. She has left Drinmery with the party, traveling with [[uriel-kenan|Uriel]] to learn more about [[the-one-above|the One Above]].
