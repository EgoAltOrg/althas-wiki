**Summary**: Player character. Novak Azimuth, the young head of [[house-azimuth|House Azimuth]] and bearer of the [[parashiel|Miracle of Parashiel]], traveling under his first name alone.

---

> [!note] Player character
> Novak Azimuth is a PC, not an NPC. He revealed his name and House in [[drinmery|Drinmery]] on Adventus 12, 366 VR.

## Appearance

A tall, athletic young man of pale complexion, with wavy white hair and sky-blue eyes, often shadowed by faint dark circles. He carries himself with a stern, high-headed bearing.

![[mystery-pc-fullbody.jpg|360]]

## Personality

He speaks in a low, strong voice. Once shy, he has grown bitter, disdainful and wary of anyone he does not fully trust. His mother's memory has hardened in him into righteousness and an unbending certainty, and grief has made him more and more authoritarian. He hates [[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]], before whom his mother fell, above all others, and he despises his uncle [[lorkhan-olnir|Lorkhan Olnir]] for the low manner of his father's death.

## Abilities

Novak bears the [[parashiel|Miracle of Parashiel]], the Miracle of [[house-azimuth|House Azimuth]].

### Character

Ancestry: [[human|Human]]. Community: Highborne. Class: Warrior, "Call of the Slayer".

## History

Novak is the firstborn of [[cornelia-azimuth|Cornelia]] and [[victerius-azimuth|Victerius]] of House Azimuth, born in [[voldaen|Voldaen]] in 345 VR. Both his parents fell in 358 VR in the war against [[jesthaen|Jesthaen]], and he took up the leadership of the House young.

### Drinmery

On Adventus 12, 366 VR, Novak came into [[drinmery|Drinmery]] through the north checkpoint with [[zalmir-aldarson|Zalmir]] and his counselor [[leonard-moore|Leonard Moore]]. Bandits tried to rob him in the market and failed; later that day they stole his mother's ring from Leonard. That night, at the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]], he revealed that he is Novak Azimuth. In the convent cellar he paid the toll of the seal on Saint [[trefan|Trefan]]'s tomb with what he knew of Parashiel, and Father [[tobias-wren|Tobias]] told him that his uncle Lorkhan had quartered his men in Drinmery during the war.

Leonard told him that House Azimuth holds a [[the-threnodies|Threnody]], which Cornelia spent much time researching, and that he believed it was what led to her experiments with Parashiel. At dawn on Adventus 15 the party attacked the bandits' fort at the [[lampfort|Lampfort]], and Novak killed its leader almost at once. When they returned on Adventus 16, they found Leonard hanged in Drinmery's town square, with Cornelia's ring on his hand.
