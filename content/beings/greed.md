**Summary**: A being of red robes and many hands who killed the gravekeeper [[aldous-pike|Aldous Pike]] in the churchyard of the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]], and who now walks in the headless body of [[ambrose-mourne|Ambrose Mourne]].

---

## Nature

### Appearance

A tall figure in layer on layer of red robes that darken to black at the hem. Pale hands reach out of the folds on every side, and several grip a long, thin spear. Where a face should be, a crown of pale hands points outward, set with staring red eyes.

## History

On Adventus 25, 366 VR, in wind and rain, Greed came to the churchyard of the Convent of Saint Trefan in [[drinmery|Drinmery]] and killed the gravekeeper, Aldous Pike, whose head burst. Greed then entered the headless body of Ambrose Mourne, who had died in that churchyard nine days before, and the body walked again. Greed wears that body now.
