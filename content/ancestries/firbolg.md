## Overview

The Firbolg are a tall, bovine-featured people native to the [[witherwild-continent|Witherwild]]. Those who live on Althas came not by choice: their forebears were carried off the Witherwild in bondage to work the southern vineyards, and their descendants, freed generations ago, have made a home among the [[caio|Caio]] caravans of [[hilltop|Hilltop]], two inheritances, road and homeland, carried in one people.

## Appearance

Firbolg are bovine humanoids: broad-nosed, with long drooping ears and faces that blend the human with the ox, cow, or bison. Some, called minotaurs, have heads wholly of cattle. They stand tall, five to seven feet, powerfully built and remarkably strong at any age, and many bear horns they are not shy to lower and charge behind. All are covered in fur, some in the muted browns and greys of ploughland, others in soft pastel pinks and blues. They live roughly a century and a half.

## Culture

Two inheritances are carried in one people. From the road the Firbolg have taken the **Dromardo**, the ethic of the open road, and the craft of the **drabardi** fortune-tellers; from the [[witherwild-continent|Witherwild]] they have kept the older [[the-faunus|Wickling ways]], the faith of their homeland, which they never wholly set down. To the [[caio|Caio]] they are proof of what that people most believes about itself: that the open road can give the discarded a home when no throne nor altar will.

## History

### From the Witherwild to the vineyards

A [[witherwild-continent|Witherwild]] people by origin, the Firbolg were carried off that continent by the colonizers of [[haven|Haven]] and set to work the vineyards of the [[serracorte|Serracorte]] in bondage, an enslaved labor-class whose work raised the wine that made [[andaluria|Andaluria]] rich.

### Emancipation and the road

When slavery was at last outlawed, the freed Firbolg found the settled country had little place for them, and the [[caio|Caio]] caravans that had always passed through the vineyard highlands did. Generations on, the Firbolg of [[hilltop|Hilltop]] are Caio in nearly everything, keeping the Dromardo and the drabardi's craft beside the [[the-faunus|Wickling ways]] they never set down.
