## Overview

Elves are among the longest-lived peoples of Althas, and in the Divine Age they were longer-lived still: while [[the-one-above|the One Above]] walked the world, the eldest of them did not die of age at all. That grace faded when the god departed. The Elves of the present are merely long-lived, not deathless, and the oldest among them remember, or half-remember, a time when their years had no end.
