**Summary**: Ambrose Mourne, last of [[house-mourne|House Mourne]], who murdered the priest of the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]] in [[drinmery|Drinmery]] and lived nearly thirty years under his name, Father Tobias Wren. Beheaded in the convent churchyard on the night of Adventus 16, 366 VR.

---

## Appearance

An old man worn gaunt and hollow-cheeked, with long, unbound hair gone pale and fine with age and deep-set, shadowed eyes. He kept the plain dark robes of a country convent priest and a simple pale ruff at the throat, and his hands were long and pale.

## Personality

Frail as he was, his manner was gentle and unhurried. Patient and warm. He knew every family's troubles, blessed the newborns, sat with the dying and turned no one away, and the town loved him for it.

## History

Father Tobias was the priest of the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]] from before the [[jesthaen|Jesthaen]] war, and the only priest it kept. He received [[malak|Malak's]] sealed package on Adventus 12, 366 VR, and tended the guardsman [[pell|Pell]] after bandits beat him in the market. From him the party learned of [[lorkhan-olnir|Lorkhan Olnir's]] time in [[drinmery|Drinmery]] during the war.

On the night of Adventus 12, [[gideon|Gideon]] dug up the unmarked grave in the convent churchyard while [[malak|Malak]] watched, and found a body with a locket: inside, an old picture worn away by time, and the initials TW. When [[rosestripe|Rosestripe]] told Father Tobias of it, he asked her where such a thing had been found. He told her, and later [[novak-azimuth|Novak]], that he meant to leave Drinmery for [[andaluria|Andaluria]] to right his past wrongs, as soon as his affairs were in order, and he showed Novak the [[the-threnodies|Threnody]] Malak had delivered. [[alys|Alys]] told the party that at her son Colm's funeral, Father Tobias put his ear to the boy's mouth and passed his last words on to her.

On the night of Adventus 16, 366 VR, he did the dying rites over [[leonard-moore|Leonard Moore]] and gave [[novak-azimuth|Novak]] Leonard's last words. When [[immanuel-greene|Greene's]] men came up the hill with [[gideon|Gideon]], Gideon named him at the churchyard gate: Ambrose Mourne, last of [[house-mourne|House Mourne]], excommunicated for heresy. He accused him of murdering the true Tobias Wren on the road nearly thirty years before and taking his name, and Ambrose admitted it. When the fight turned against him, he raised the churchyard dead and broke a sealed letter, which rose as a pillar of light and wrapped him in a holy shield. A wolf spirit and [[rosestripe|Rosestripe]] broke through it, and the wolf beheaded him. Gideon took his head for the [[the-holy-see|Holy See]].

On Adventus 25, 366 VR, [[greed|Greed]] came to the convent churchyard, killed the gravekeeper [[aldous-pike|Aldous Pike]], and entered Ambrose's headless body. The body walked again, and Greed wears it now.
