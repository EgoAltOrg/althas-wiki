**Summary**: A wanderer who looks to the party like a man made of stars, traveling companion of [[d-the-bard|D, the Bard]].

---

## Appearance

To the party he looks like a man made of stars: a figure of night sky, star-flecked, in the shape of a man.

## History

The party met Caz at the door of [[fustierhall|Fustierhall]] on Adventus 25, 366 VR. He told them he is cursed to look that way, but only to certain people. He had come up the hill after a caravan that went into the manor, and he went in without the party. He has told them nothing of where he comes from.
