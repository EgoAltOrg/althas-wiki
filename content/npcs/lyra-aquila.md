**Summary**: Lyra Aquila, Archmage of the Triumvirate of [[polaris|Polaris]] and twin sister to [[cassio-aquila|Cassio Aquila]]. One of the [[the-five-heroes|Five Heroes]] who brought down [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] in 334 VR, bearer of the [[miracles|Miracle of Anamnesis]], and author of *Verba Ophanim*. Seldom seen in public now.

---

## Abilities

### Her Miracles

Lyra was born with the [[miracles|Miracle of Anamnesis]]: she forgets nothing she has perceived, holding every seal she has read and every word she has heard perfect and permanent. Because she and her twin [[cassio-aquila|Cassio]] were each born with a different Miracle, she also held his [[miracles|Miracle of Pentecost]] as fully as her own, and so, while he lived, understood any tongue or notation the instant she met it. Between memory and understanding she was among the foremost minds of [[codex-magic|Codex]] scholarship in Polaris: no seal was ever foreign to her, and none she read was ever lost.

## History

Lyra Aquila is one of the [[the-five-heroes|Five Heroes]] who fought and brought down [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] at [[hilltop|Hilltop]] in 333-334 VR, an Archmage of the Triumvirate of Polaris (see [[polaris|Polaris]]), and, with her twin brother [[cassio-aquila|Cassio Aquila]], one of the two bearers of [[house-aquila|House Aquila's]] Miracles.

### Verba Ophanim

Lyra alone could have written *Verba Ophanim*, the account of the [[the-ophanim|Ophanim]] from its coming to its dying words. Pentecost let her understand a Herald no other witness could parse, and Anamnesis let her set every word of it down without error. The book is her testimony to the thing the Five Heroes killed, and it is how most of Althas knows the Ophanim's story at all.

### Her withdrawal

When [[cassio-aquila|Cassio]] died bringing the Ophanim down in 334 VR, Lyra withdrew. In the decades since she has descended from her tower only rarely, a powerful and aging Archmage absorbed in work she shares with no one; whether grief, her [[the-ophanim|OphDreams]], or only her studies keeps her secluded, no one outside her circle can say. The [[house-aquila|House Aquila]] she and her brother had made the proudest answer to Voldaen fell quiet with them.
