**Summary**: A young guardsman of [[drinmery|Drinmery]], once of Greene's company, now squire to [[novak-azimuth|Novak Azimuth]].

---

## Appearance

A young man with shoulder-length fair hair, in a high-collared coat over mail, a sword at his hip.

## History

Pell stands [[drinmery|Drinmery's]] checkpoints. During the market raid on Adventus 12, 366 VR, bandits beat him to the ground as a distraction, and Father [[ambrose-mourne|Tobias]] healed his bruised ribs. He told [[zalmir-aldarson|Zalmir]] that the bandits' blades look like barracks steel, all from the same smith.

He was one of [[immanuel-greene|Greene's]] men in the battle at the convent on the night of Adventus 16, and with the smith [[garrick-thorne|Garrick Thorne]] the only one of Greene's company to survive it. The next morning he asked [[novak-azimuth|Novak]] to take him as his squire, and Novak agreed. He travels with the party to [[jestha|Jestha]].
