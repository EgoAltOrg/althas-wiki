**Summary**: **Castus Voldis**, King of [[voldaen|Voldaen]] (r. 149-180 VR), son of [[valeran-voldis|Valeran]] the Blessed King and the monarch under whom the north partitioned away peacefully to become [[polaris|Polaris]] by [[the-lodestar-pact|the Lodestar Pact]] in 167 VR. Elder brother of the prince Castorius, who renounced his claim in that settlement.

---

## History

Castus of [[house-voldis|House Voldis]], son and heir of [[valeran-voldis|Valeran]] the Blessed King, held the crown of [[voldaen|Voldaen]] when the northern academies and mages sought their independence. Rather than the bloodshed of [[valerions-heresy|Valerion's Heresy]] half a century before, his reign settled the split by treaty: [[the-lodestar-pact|the Lodestar Pact]] of 167 VR, out of which [[polaris|Polaris]] was founded. His younger brother, the prince Castorius, renounced his own claim as part of that settlement and afterward passed out of the record.
