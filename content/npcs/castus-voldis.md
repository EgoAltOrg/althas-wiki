**Summary**: **Castus Voldis**, King of [[voldaen|Voldaen]] (r. 165-221 VR) and son of [[valeran-voldis|Valeran]] the Blessed King. In his reign the north partitioned away peacefully to become [[polaris|Polaris]] by [[the-lodestar-pact|the Lodestar Pact]] in 167 VR, and the colony of [[haven|Haven]] was planted. Elder twin of the prince Castorius, who renounced his claim in that settlement. He died at Haven's fall defending its colonists, after slaying the great serpent Shun'Aush.

## History

Castus of [[house-voldis|House Voldis]], son and heir of [[valeran-voldis|Valeran]] the Blessed King, held the crown of [[voldaen|Voldaen]] when the northern academies and mages sought their independence. Rather than the bloodshed of [[valerions-heresy|Valerion's Heresy]] half a century before, his reign settled the split by treaty: [[the-lodestar-pact|the Lodestar Pact]] of 167 VR, out of which [[polaris|Polaris]] was founded. His younger brother, the prince Castorius, renounced his own claim as part of that settlement and afterward passed out of the record.

Castus and Castorius were twins, and their father gave each of them one of his Twinblades, the swords Truth and Ideal; Castorius gave his up with his claim, and Castus carried both. In his reign the three powers planted the colony of [[haven|Haven]] in the [[witherwild-continent|Witherwild]], and in 221 VR he crossed to it on a royal visit. When the great serpent [[the-faunus|Faunus]] Shun'Aush fell on the colony, Castus slew it, and when Haven collapsed under plague and the wild soon after, he died holding the line while its colonists fled. The Twinblades were lost with him. Common speech calls the venture Castus's Folly, remembering him both as the king who let Haven be planted and as the king who died saving its people.
