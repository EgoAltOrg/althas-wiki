**Summary**: **Ezekiel Ashmark**, the Infernis Archbishop of [[jesthaen|Jesthaen]], who heads the [[parish-of-orthodoxy|Parish of Orthodoxy]] in the Republic and reports to the Pontiff alone. He was [[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]]'s right hand in the civil war, and he bears the [[miracles|Miracle of Raptus]].

---

## Appearance

A tall Infernis with deep blue skin, gold eyes, swept-back dark hair and a long tail. He wears the red and black of his office and carries a slender sword at his hip.

## Personality

He is devout and soft-spoken, and would rather mend a wound than open one. The people of Jestha know him as a priest first; to the soldiers of the civil war he was one of the revolution's leaders.

## Abilities

### The Miracle of Raptus

Ezekiel bears the [[miracles|Miracle of Raptus]], which lets him step from one place to another without crossing the space between, carrying with him whoever he holds. He kept it to himself through the civil war and revealed it only when his elevation was in question. A Miracle means holyblood, and holyblood answered the loudest objection to an Infernis Archbishop.

## History

### The basilica

Before the civil war he ran the infirmary of the [[basilica-of-saint-avel|Basilica of Saint Avel]], in the city now called [[jestha|Jestha]], where one of its priests had taken him in as a young man.

### The civil war

When the revolution broke out in 351 VR he went to [[aldric-voldis|Aldric]], became one of its leaders, and rose to be Aldric's right hand.

### The Ashmark

Soldiers of [[voldaen|Voldaen]] still tell of a night before a battle when every officer in their camp woke with a cross of ash on his brow, though the sentries swore no one had passed. The officers would not march, and the army withdrew without a fight, so the battle never counted against [[augustus-corvus|the Red Raven]]'s unbroken record. Jesthaen's soldiers named him for that night, and he took the name as his own.

### Archbishop of Jesthaen

After the war [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]] raised him to Archbishop of Jesthaen, head of the [[parish-of-orthodoxy|Parish of Orthodoxy]] in the Republic, reporting to the Pontiff alone. His people are banned from most settlements of Althas for their supposed association with [[the-ones-below|the Ones Below]] (see [[infernis|Infernis]]), so an Infernis in so high an office is rare.

## Relationships

- **[[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]]**: his commander in the civil war, whose right hand he became.
