**Summary**: A Caio fiddler the party met at the crossroads before [[treveth|Treveth]], traveling companion of [[caz-the-wanderer|Caz]].

---

## Appearance

A young man with untidy black hair and blue-green eyes, in a dark coat with silver braid, his instrument slung across his back.

## History

The party found D on Adventus 25, 366 VR, at the crossroads where the road to the border towns parts from the old road to Treveth, beside the empty wagons of an Infernis caravan. He sang them both songs of [[treveth|Treveth]]. He told [[novak-azimuth|Novak]] that he knows his grandmother, [[zhenya-azimuth|Zhenya Azimuth]], and that he once played at [[house-azimuth|House Azimuth]], where he saw Novak as a child. Novak noticed that D looks barely older than he is. D told them his companion Caz had gone up to the manor after the caravan, and talked the party into going to help.
