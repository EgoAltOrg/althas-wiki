**Summary**: The intrepid, martial one of the twin daughters of [[cornelia-azimuth|Cornelia]] and [[victerius-azimuth|Victerius]] of [[house-azimuth|House Azimuth]], twin to [[hestia-azimuth|Hestia]] and younger sister to [[novak-azimuth|Novak]]. Trained in both [[codex-magic|Codex]] and [[splendor-magic|Splendor]] magic, and devoted to her brother.

---

## Personality

Of the two, Agathia is the singularly intrepid one, ever favoring training in martial prowess, where [[hestia-azimuth|Hestia]] prefers strategy and quiet. For all that distinguishes them, the sisters are close and trusting, both good-natured and strong-tempered. Since the death of their parents, Agathia and Hestia have leaned on Novak, who took up the role of their protector, and they continue their own cultivation in the hope of aiding him however they can.

## History

Born in [[voldaen|Voldaen]] in 350 VR, Agathia and her twin [[hestia-azimuth|Hestia]] are the daughters of [[cornelia-azimuth|Cornelia]] and [[victerius-azimuth|Victerius]] of [[house-azimuth|House Azimuth]]. From early childhood the twins were educated and trained with the same rigor and breadth as their elder brother [[novak-azimuth|Novak]], on whom they dote and who returns the fondness. Unlike Novak, their upbringing leaned heavily on the cultivation of both [[codex-magic|Codex]] and [[splendor-magic|Splendor]] magic. They share their father's charisma and flaxen hair, and their mother's regal grace, virtuousness, and bright blue eyes.
