**Summary**: An Abbot of [[the-holy-see|the Holy See's]] Parish of Cartography, blessed with the [[miracles|Miracle]] of Geosensus, seated as the Surveyor on [[andaluria|Andaluria's]] [[the-council-of-six|Council of Six]].

---

## Appearance

An older elf with dark skin, warm amber eyes, and long silver-white hair worn in thick braids. She dresses in the white and gold of her office, marked by a heavy golden medallion.

## Personality

A dutiful official who carries out her charge without complaint, and quietly wishes it were larger than it is. A place in the Parish was set aside for her before she was ever old enough to choose one, and she has served it faithfully while privately dreaming of doing something that matters beyond the maps.

The Miracle she carries is the one known to run in [[house-circinus|House Circinus]], so she is taken to be of that oldblood house, though she came up in the Parish's service rather than the house's halls.

## The Miracle of Geosensus

Sabara's Miracle lets her feel the natural relief of the land around her within a radius: the mountains, valleys, and caves of wherever she stands. It is precise enough to map ground she cannot see and useful enough that the Parish of Cartography claimed her for it, but it reaches only so far and reads only stone and shape, never more.

## Role

The Holy See set Sabara to study how the land itself was changed by the war and [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]], in the name of the Parish of Cartography. Because that work carries her through dangerous country, the See requires her to bring Penitents along to guard the towns she passes, so she can keep to her surveying.

That charge is also her seat. The See placed her on [[andaluria|Andaluria's]] [[the-council-of-six|Council of Six]] as its Surveyor, the office that holds the region's terrain and judges where its war-changed ground can safely be rebuilt, the sixth seat added to the five hereditary offices.
