## History

Jory Fenn was one of the bandits who raided the [[drinmery|Drinmery]] [[drinmery-market|market]] on Adventus 12, 366 VR. The party let him go on his word never to return to the gang, but he went back to their fort west along the river. When the party attacked the fort he was one of three bandits who surrendered, and the only one spared, so that he could testify against General [[immanuel-greene|Greene]]. He told them Greene had first pick of any loot the gang stole, and that [[cornelia-azimuth|Cornelia Azimuth's]] ring was never at the fort. After Greene's death he left Drinmery with the party and Syndic [[yselda-marrow|Yselda Marrow]], bound for [[jestha|Jestha]] to testify.
