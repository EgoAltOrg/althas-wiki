**Summary**: **Saint Avel Cruoris**, an Elven noble of House Cruoris and bearer of the [[miracles|Miracle of Viaticum]], who stopped to tend the wounded of [[valerions-heresy|Valerion's Heresy]] and founded what became the [[basilica-of-saint-avel|Basilica of Saint Avel]]. Missing and presumed dead since [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] descended on [[hilltop|Hilltop]] in 333 VR.

---

## Appearance

Her only likeness is a stained-glass window in the original infirmary of her basilica: a pale-haired figure in dark robes, hands joined in prayer against red glass.

## Personality

Avel was warm with people and patient with criticism: she could hear out a long objection while she went on preparing a dressing, and hold calmly to her choice to care for those in need. She wanted to give each person the chance to live and to change.

## Abilities

### The Miracle of Viaticum

Avel bore her house's [[miracles|Miracle of Viaticum]] (see [[amalthus-cruoris|Amalthus Cruoris]]). She used it to carry care beyond her presence, sealing the [[splendor-magic|Splendor]] healing prayers she knew into her own holyblood, so that a single working could reach a patient she had left behind. As with any incantation, the correct words and their exact saying were still required.

## History

### The saint who stayed

During [[valerions-heresy|Valerion's Heresy]] (93 to 117 VR), Avel was an itinerant healer who treated the wounded whatever side they had fought for. She stopped to care for people too weak to travel, kept postponing her departure, planted a garden, prepared beds and taught others to help her. The infirmary became a community, and she gave a long part of her life to it. Her disciples continued the work and built the basilica around her small infirmary.

### Saint in her lifetime

Her patients and their families began calling her a saint while she lived, and the Church recognized her during her lifetime too, which brought visitors, donations and responsibilities to her work.

Recognition did not end the disagreement around her. Some within the Church demanded signs of repentance before a Sinner received the same care as anyone else; Avel treated the wounded before that first step. Others objected that she spent the holyblood of Viaticum on people who scorned its source. Her reputation spread faster than her critics could answer it, but she kept working at the bedside.

### Disappearance

In 333 VR, during an ordinary stay with her house in [[hilltop|Hilltop]], Avel disappeared when [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] descended. She is presumed dead, and no certainty about her final moments ever reached the house she founded.

## Relationships

- **House Cruoris**: her house, the line of the Miracle of Viaticum that has given the Church several Pontiffs, among them [[amalthus-cruoris|Amalthus Cruoris]].
