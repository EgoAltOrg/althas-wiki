**Summary**: A Half-Elf Syndic of the [[guild|Guild]], who worked in [[drinmery|Drinmery]] under cover as a clerk of the Merchant's Guild Courthouse.

---

## History

After [[immanuel-greene|Greene's]] death on Adventus 16, 366 VR, she let the party know she is a Syndic of the Guild. She travels with them and [[jory-fenn|Jory Fenn]] to [[jestha|Jestha]], so that Jory can testify against Greene.
