**Summary**: Bram Tolliver, Marshal of [[jestha|Jestha]] and commander of its watch, a former southern rebel whom [[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]] appointed despite his past.

---

## History

Before the revolution, Bram Tolliver ran with one of the town crews that [[the-freehands|the Freehands]] later grew from, the thieves who preyed on [[voldaen|Voldaen]]'s nobility. He killed a debt-lord in his own counting house, was caught, and was given [[the-witherwatch|the Witherwatch]] in place of a noose. He served there under [[aldric-voldis|Aldric]], followed him south, and fought through the revolution and the war that followed.

After the peace of 361 VR, Aldric made him Marshal of [[jestha|Jestha]], commander of the capital's watch. No one has forgotten what he was. Aldric's critics say he set a killer to catch killers, and the Freehands who refused the peace count Bram among the worst of the traitors: a man who was one of them and now hunts them for the state.

## Relationships

- **[[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]]**: his commander on the Witherwatch, who made him Marshal of Jestha and is criticized for it.
- **[[the-freehands|The Freehands]]**: his old movement; the extremists who kept the name call him a traitor.
