**Summary**: **Antares Algol**, Archmage of the Triumvirate of [[polaris|Polaris]] and bearer of House Algol's [[miracles|Miracle of Abrogation]]. With [[hesper-arcturus|Hesper Arcturus]]'s seat empty since her death and [[lyra-aquila|Lyra Aquila]] seldom leaving her tower, he is in practice the power that governs Polaris.

---

## Abilities

### The Miracle of Abrogation

Antares bears House Algol's signature [[miracles|Miracle]], Abrogation. For a moment it cuts a caster off from the power their magic draws on, so the spell fails before it can take effect. It reaches only as far as he can see, and it cannot touch a Miracle, whose power comes from its bearer.

## History

### Archmage of Polaris

Antares holds one of the three seats of Polaris's Triumvirate of Archmages. The second seat, [[hesper-arcturus|Hesper Arcturus]]'s, has stood empty since she was killed in 363 VR, the death that set off the Errai Riots, and no trial has yet chosen her successor. The other Archmage, [[lyra-aquila|Lyra Aquila]], has kept to her tower for decades and rarely comes down. In practice, Antares is the one who governs.
