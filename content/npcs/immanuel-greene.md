**Summary**: A lord of [[jesthaen|Jesthaen]] whose hired guard keeps the peace in [[drinmery|Drinmery]], called "General" by his men.

---

## Appearance

A huge man, broad as a doorway, who carries a war hammer on his shoulder.

## Personality

Curt and direct, a man of few words.

## History

After bandits raided the [[drinmery|Drinmery]] market on Adventus 12, 366 VR, Greene arrived with his men and laid a dead bandit, one he had run down himself, at the party's feet by way of introduction. He kept one captured bandit at the barracks, let the party keep another, and offered [[novak-azimuth|Novak]] the chance to question his prisoner if it would help recover the stolen ring.

The bandits never attacked his carriages, only other merchants', as [[mother-wenna|Mother Wenna]] told [[uriel-kenan|Uriel]]. [[jory-fenn|Jory Fenn]], a bandit the party spared at the fort, said Greene had first pick of all the gang's stolen loot, and that [[cornelia-azimuth|Cornelia's]] ring was never at the fort.
