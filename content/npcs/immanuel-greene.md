**Summary**: A veteran of [[jesthaen|Jesthaen]]'s revolution whose hired guard kept the peace in [[drinmery|Drinmery]], called "General" by his men. Killed at the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]] on the night of Adventus 16, 366 VR.

---

## Appearance

A huge man, broad as a doorway, who carried a war hammer on his shoulder.

## Personality

Curt and direct, a man of few words.

## History

After bandits raided the [[drinmery|Drinmery]] market on Adventus 12, 366 VR, Greene arrived with his men and laid a dead bandit, one he had run down himself, at the party's feet by way of introduction. He kept one captured bandit at the barracks, let the party keep another, and offered [[novak-azimuth|Novak]] the chance to question his prisoner if it would help recover the stolen ring.

The bandits never attacked his carriages, only other merchants', as Mother [[wenna|Wenna]] told [[uriel-kenan|Uriel]]. [[jory-fenn|Jory Fenn]], a bandit the party spared at the fort, said Greene had first pick of all the gang's stolen loot, and that [[cornelia-azimuth|Cornelia's]] ring was never at the fort.

On the night of Adventus 16 he led his men up the convent hill with [[gideon|Gideon]] to take the priest. His company was winning against the party when the churchyard dead rose, and Greene's men and the party fought the dead together. The dead killed him, and the priest raised him again at once, but he fell for good when the priest died. Of his company only [[pell|Pell]] and the barracks smith, [[garrick-thorne|Garrick Thorne]], survived. [[rastaban|Ras]] took his warhammer. His body was later found desecrated, its eyes, ears and hands cut away.
