**Summary**: Saint Trefan, who held the [[lampfort|Lampfort]] for [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]] in [[valerions-heresy|Valerion's Heresy]] and afterward built the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]] in [[drinmery|Drinmery]], where he lies entombed.

---

## History

During [[valerions-heresy|Valerion's Heresy]], Trefan held the [[lampfort|Lampfort]] for [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]] against the Mad King's host with only a handful of men. Each night they lit a lamp in every window and carried them from room to room, so that the crown's scouts counted a garrison of hundreds. The host dug in for a siege instead of storming the walls, and that bought enough time for the See's reinforcements to arrive. After the war he built the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]] in [[drinmery|Drinmery]], which bears his name.

His tomb lies at the end of the convent cellar, behind an illusory wall held by a [[codex-magic|Codex]] seal. To open it, the seal takes what the opener knows of one creature and writes it into Saint Trefan's Lorekeeper, a book left behind in the tomb. When the party opened it on Adventus 12, 366 VR, the tomb had already been broken into, the saint's body desecrated and its brain removed.
