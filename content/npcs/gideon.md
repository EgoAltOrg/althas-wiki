**Summary**: A young, low-ranking [[parish-of-inquisition|Inquisitor]] lately come to [[drinmery|Drinmery]] to rest a wounded hand, and to stand the [[the-holy-see|Sleepless Vigil]] beside its priest.

---

## Appearance

A young man with the bearing of a soldier more than a cleric, dark hair swept back from a face scarred twice. One eye is clouded and scarred, a wound of the [[jesthaen|Jesthaen]] war; the mouth is cut through by a knife-scar that never healed clean, and it drags his speech into a slur he plainly hates. He wears mail under a dark cloak clasped with gold, and keeps his right hand bound and favored, the injury he says has brought him to [[drinmery|Drinmery]] to rest. He speaks little and flatly, like a man more used to giving orders than to talking.

## Personality

Brutal, exact and quick to anger. Gideon follows an order to the letter without questioning it, and has no patience for anyone slow, evasive or wasting his time. The faith teaches gentleness, and he is bad at faking it.

## Abilities

Gideon bears no [[miracles|Miracle]] and none of the holyblood that opens the faith's higher offices. He is a commoner who rose through obedience and a strong arm. What he has instead is a soldier's training and an [[parish-of-inquisition|Inquisitor's]] writ, which lets him question, judge, and declare heresy where he finds it.

## History

### Drinmery

Gideon came to [[drinmery|Drinmery]] to rest a wounded hand, and keeps the [[the-holy-see|Sleepless Vigil]] at the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]] in turns with [[tobias-wren|Father Tobias]]. He held the Sinner [[rastaban|Rastaban]] until the Inquisitor [[uriel-kenan|Uriel Kenan]] came to take him, and warned Uriel that his own last Sinner had tried to kill him in his sleep. He has no patience for Penitents, and he and Uriel disagree on how far the Holy See's authority should reach in other nations. He has remarked that a convent the size of Saint Trefan's keeps a single priest, and that the priest takes less interest in the town's law than a priest should. Not long after his arrival, he killed two men caught trying to rape a woman.

On the night of Adventus 12, 366 VR, he dug up the unmarked grave in the convent churchyard, where [[malak|Malak]] found him, and found a body with a locket bearing the initials TW. When Father Tobias began preparing to leave Drinmery, Gideon was sure Malak had let it slip, and was furious with him. He and [[uriel-kenan|Uriel]] talked at length about their differing philosophies as Inquisitors, and he realized Uriel was a religious extremist: loyal to [[lael|Lael]] rather than to Pontiff [[amalthus-cruoris|Amalthus]], his memories wiped, and convinced that the holy purge that began with [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] must be completed by Inquisitors' own hands. He called Uriel a cultist, but agreed they shared two goals, the bandits and Father Tobias. He rode with the party to the bandits' fort. On the way back he gave Uriel his sword, asked the party to tell Father Tobias he had died fighting the bandits, and left them, promising to meet them in town.
