**Summary**: **Haven**, the colony three Althan powers raised in [[witherwild-continent|the Witherwild]] generations ago and abandoned after the great serpent Shun'Aush fell on it. Remembered across Althas as Castus's Folly, its ruin is the reason the [[the-witherwatch|Witherwatch]] stands.

---

## Overview

Common speech remembers the whole venture as Castus's Folly, after the king who permitted it and died defending it: a doomed reach for a land that could not be held. What became of Haven after the exodus, no one in Althas can say. Some tell that a few colonists refused to leave, and that they may be living there still.

## History

Generations ago, in the years after the [[the-lodestar-pact|Lodestar Pact]], [[voldaen|Voldaen]], [[polaris|Polaris]], and [[hilltop|Hilltop]] joined to plant a colony beyond the northwest pass, in the untamed [[witherwild-continent|Witherwild]]. They called it Haven. For a time it prospered. Its colonists prized a rare red flower of the deep wilds, one bloom in ten thousand. Its Wick name was set into Althan speech by the same [[house-aquila|House Aquila]] missionary who named the Faunus: the **Crimson Lady's Veil**. The flower was known to have curative properties rivaling those of the most powerful [[splendor-magic|Splendor]] incantations.

In 221 VR King [[castus-voldis|Castus]] came on a royal visit, and in those days the great serpent [[the-faunus|Faunus]] Shun'Aush fell on the colony. Castus died fighting it, and the serpent fell with him. The colonists abandoned Haven soon after, and the growth they had failed to tame began to creep toward Althas's own border, which is why the [[the-witherwatch|Witherwatch]] was raised to hold the [[godless-gate|Gate]].

Some of those who came home carried a wasting the colonists had named the Serpent's Sickness, and it still breaks out in Althas from time to time. The most recent outbreak was cured by Betur Vitario, a world-renowned doctor from [[polaris|Polaris]].
