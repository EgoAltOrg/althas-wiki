**Summary**: **The Godless Gate**, the mountain pass that joins Althas to [[witherwild-continent|the Witherwild]] in the northwest, and the one way between the two. The [[the-witherwatch|Witherwatch]] garrisons it, holding the blighted frontier's wildlife back from Althas.

---

## Overview

The Godless Gate is the high pass through which alone Althas and [[witherwild-continent|the Witherwild]] are joined, the pass itself and the tunnels bored through the rock beneath it, cut where the northwestern range brings the two landmasses together. Through the Gate came the growth and the beasts that spilled out of the dying Witherwild, and at the Gate the [[the-witherwatch|Witherwatch]] was set to stop them. To hold the Gate is to hold the only door the blight can walk through.
