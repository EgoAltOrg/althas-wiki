**Summary**: **The Witherwild**, the blighted continent joined to Althas by a single mountain pass in the northwest. A hostile frontier of unending growth gone wrong, it is known to most Althans only as the grave of [[haven|Haven]], the doomed colony remembered as "the Folly," and as the land the [[the-witherwatch|Witherwatch]] holds the border against.

---

## Overview

The Witherwild is the second landmass of the known world, reachable from Althas only through the [[godless-gate|Godless Gate]], the pass that joins the two in the northwest. Where Althas is settled and mapped, the Witherwild is neither: a wilderness of choking, endless growth, its seasons stalled, its wildlife swollen and hostile, pressing forever outward against any border set to hold it.

What Althas remembers of it is [[haven|Haven]], the colony three of its powers raised there generations ago and lost to plague and the wild. Since that ruin, called "the Folly" in common speech, the Witherwild has been a place to be walled out rather than walked into, and the [[the-witherwatch|Witherwatch]] keeps the [[godless-gate|Gate]] shut against what still comes out of it.

### A land of rumor

Few Althans have crossed the [[godless-gate|Godless Gate]], and fewer have come back, so most of what the mainland holds about the Witherwild is rumor rather than fact. The tales come from the colonists of [[haven|Haven]], from before the Folly, and from [[the-witherwatch|Witherwatch]] veterans since, and they grow taller the farther from the Gate they are told:

- That its days and nights each last weeks, the sun holding the sky seven days and more before a night just as long, past anything an Althan calendar can keep.
- That its beasts grow to monstrous size, its plants hunt and strangle, and nothing there ever stops growing.
- That the Wicklings share their villages with living spirits they call gods, met on the road in the flesh, as ready to curse as to bless.
- That the ground itself is holy and cursed, and a grey range the size of mountains is the body of a dead god the colony was mad to quarry.

Ruin never killed the dream the Folly was built on. Highborn children across the five nations still grow up on stories of the Witherwild and its lost riches, and more than a few swear they will be the one to lead its reconquest and succeed where Haven died, the same hunger that sends other men chasing the lost glories of the Divine Age.

## Inhabitants

### The Faunus

The Wicklings share the Fanewick with the Faunus, beings they hold to be gods that walk the land in the flesh and trade small miracles for worship as readily as they punish with tricks and curses. What little of that faith reached Althas came filtered through the [[house-aquila|House Aquila]] missionary who led the [[the-holy-see|Holy See's]] part of the [[haven|Haven]] colony: bearing the [[miracles|Miracle of Pentecost]], they alone could understand the Wicklings, and it was they who named the beings the **Faunus** and fixed the forms of their names the mainland now uses. The faith Althas reconstructed from those thin records, and the handful of named Faunus it still remembers (the Great Owl Nikta chief among them), are gathered on the Faunus.

### The changed peoples

The blight does not spare the Witherwild's peoples. Witherwatch scouts and the odd returning traveler bring back reports of **Drakona** in the deep wild, more than survive anywhere in Althas and stranger, their horns longer and their shapes nearer the dragons of legend; Althan scholars guess they are refugees of the Divine-Age purge that scattered the [[drakona|Drakona]], but no one can say for certain. The same reports tell of **fungril**, the mushroom-folk, grown far past the size of their scattered Althan kin. How much is true and how much the tale-telling of frightened men, no one in Althas is placed to know.
