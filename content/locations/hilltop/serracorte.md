## Overview

The Serracorte is the volcanic upland that gives [[andaluria|Andaluria]] its character and its wealth. Its northern volcanoes are dormant. Sirocco winds cross dark volcanic soil, and the vineyards on its slopes yield the most coveted wine in the region and Andaluria's chief export. The wine is carried down the Valmorria river to the capital, Jesaña, on the southern coast, and traded on, begrudgingly, to the now-independent [[armada|Armada]] beyond. Those slopes were once worked by [[firbolg|Firbolg]] carried off [[witherwild-continent|the Witherwild]] in bondage; freed when slavery was outlawed, most left the settled highlands for the [[caio|Caio]] caravans that pass through.

## Notable Places

Its heights are crowned by Castillo del Cardenal, the hilltop seat of the Condesa [[ysabela-delamona|Ysabela Delamoña IV]], where she keeps to herself and throws the masquerades for which Andaluria's elite abandon the darkening countryside.
