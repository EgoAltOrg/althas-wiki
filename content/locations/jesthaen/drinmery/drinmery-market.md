**Summary**: The market square at [[drinmery|Drinmery's]] east bridgehead, where most of the town's trade passes.

---

## History

On Adventus 12, 366 VR, bandits led by [[wick|Wick]] raided the market. They beat the guardsman [[pell|Pell]] and stole [[cornelia-azimuth|Cornelia Azimuth's]] ring from [[leonard-moore|Leonard Moore]], and [[immanuel-greene|Immanuel Greene]] arrived with his men after the fight. On the evening of Adventus 16 the party found Leonard hanged naked in the square, a sign reading "Death to Queensmen" around his neck.
