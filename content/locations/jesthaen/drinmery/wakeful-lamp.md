## Overview

The Wakeful Lamp is the tavern beside [[drinmery-market|the market]] at [[drinmery|Drinmery's]] bridgehead, where carters, guards and travelers drink. It is named for the lamps of the [[lampfort|Lampfort]].

## Inhabitants

- [[hollis-brand|Hollis Brand]], its keeper.

## Notable Places

- The [[restful-shade|Restful Shade]], a little-known brothel associated with the tavern.
