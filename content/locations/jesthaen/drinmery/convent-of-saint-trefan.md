**Summary**: A convent in [[drinmery|Drinmery]], home to a flock of Aetheris and prized for its rare library.

---

## Overview

Not a large convent, but its library holds a rare and valuable book collection, built up over the years, and that is its pride. Saint Trefan built it after [[valerions-heresy|Valerion's Heresy]], the war in which he made his stand at the [[lampfort|Lampfort]], and it bears his name. It draws a steady flow of visitors: church officials with business in [[drinmery|Drinmery]], and scholars coming to study.

## Notable Places

### The tomb of Saint Trefan

At the end of the convent cellar, a [[codex-magic|Codex]] seal holds an illusory wall over the tomb of Saint Trefan. To open it, the seal takes what the opener knows of one creature and writes it into Saint Trefan's Lorekeeper, a book left behind in the tomb. When the party opened it, the tomb had already been broken into, the saint's body desecrated and its brain removed. The Lorekeeper's pages now hold [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] and [[parashiel|Parashiel]]. Whoever carries the Lorekeeper gains an edge against the creatures it records: +1 to action rolls against them.

### The churchyard

![[convent-churchyard-map.jpg]]

The convent's churchyard, kept by [[aldous-pike|Aldous Pike]]. On the night of Adventus 12, 366 VR, [[gideon|Gideon]] dug up the unmarked grave there.

## Inhabitants

A flock of [[aetheris|Aetheris]] lives and works at the convent, using its library and kitchen in exchange for service as messengers and general help around the grounds.

Its one priest is [[tobias-wren|Father Tobias Wren]]. The Aetheris [[inkfeather|Inkfeather]] keeps its library, and [[aldous-pike|Aldous Pike]] tends its churchyard.
