**Summary**: A medium-sized city in [[jesthaen|Jesthaen]], best known as the home of the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]].

---

## Overview

The biggest local urban center in its stretch of [[jesthaen|Jesthaen]], and where most local trade passes through. Its most important landmark is the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]], which draws a steady stream of visitors from the church and from scholars using its library.

## History

Drinmery is wary of outsiders. During the [[jesthaen|Jesthaen]] war, [[lorkhan-olnir|Lorkhan Olnir]] quartered his men in the town and ate townsfolk, and the town has not forgotten.

## Notable Places

- The [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]], on the rise above the town, with its churchyard.
- The market at the east bridgehead.
- The Wakeful Lamp, the tavern beside the market, kept by [[hollis-brand|Hollis Brand]].
- The manned checkpoints on every road into town.

## Inhabitants

The town is held by [[guilmore-fleming|Count Fleming]], and its peace is kept by the hired guard of [[immanuel-greene|Immanuel Greene]]. Among its people:

- [[aldous-pike|Aldous Pike]], gravekeeper of the convent churchyard.
- [[alys|Alys]], a grieving mother.
- [[edric-colvin|Edric Colvin]], steward to the Count.
- [[hollis-brand|Hollis Brand]], keeper of the Wakeful Lamp.
- [[inkfeather|Inkfeather]], the convent's librarian and healer.
- [[maud-tallow|Maud Tallow]], a candle-seller in the market.
- [[pell|Pell]], a young guardsman.
- [[wick|Wick]], a bandit.
