## Overview

The Count's manor stands on a low rise overlooking the center of town.

## History

The party dined and slept here as the Count's guests on Adventus 12, 366 VR. They met [[dorothea|Dorothea]] here, and the Count paid [[malak|Malak]] for his delivery with the deed to a decommissioned merchant ship. On Adventus 13, [[leonard-moore|Leonard Moore]] left the party for the manor.

## Inhabitants

- [[guilmore-fleming|Count Fleming]], Senator of Drinmery.
- [[edric-colvin|Edric Colvin]], his steward.
