## Overview

Fustierhall stands on a hill above [[treveth|Treveth]], on the old road from Drinmery to Jestha. Travelers have left that road, because people go missing on it, and at the crossroads before Treveth they take the road to the border towns instead. The songs say it was a duke's seat. The locals call it the Larder, and they do not stop near it.

## History

The two songs sung of [[treveth|Treveth]] tell of the civil war of 351 VR: the duke barred his doors and kept his grain while the village starved, [[the-freehands|the Freehands]] came up the hill with torches, and by morning the fires were out and nobody was left. Soldiers who camped there later vanished too, and left only their boots.

On Adventus 25, 366 VR, the party came to the door, where the stranger [[caz-the-wanderer|Caz]] went in ahead of them after a caravan. A face looked at them out of the fog, and they ran inside. In the entrance hall they heard a lullaby.
