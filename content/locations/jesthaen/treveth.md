**Summary**: An empty village below [[fustierhall|Fustierhall]], on the old road from [[drinmery|Drinmery]] to [[jestha|Jestha]], which travelers no longer take because people go missing there.

---

## Overview

A village of gray stone below the hill of Fustierhall, empty and full of fog. The party searched it on Adventus 25, 366 VR, and found nothing.

## History

### The songs of Treveth

The fiddler [[d-the-bard|D]] sang both to the party at the crossroads before Treveth.

> [!quote] The Fires of Treveth (the Freehand ballad)
>
> In Treveth when the war was young,
> the duke barred fast his door,
> he kept the grain and held his tongue
> while hunger took the poor.
>
> They laid their sickles in the field,
> they would not reap his bread,
> "We'll starve as free folk ere we yield,
> we'll not be serfs," they said.
>
> *So raise a cup to Treveth's dead,*
> *who would not bend the knee,*
> *the crown has walls, the crown has bread,*
> *but Treveth's folk died free.*
>
> The Freehands heard it on the road
> and came with steel and flame,
> a blade for every hand that hoed,
> for every hand a name.
>
> They climbed up Fustier's hill by night,
> the torches burning red,
> but long before the morning light
> the Treveth fires were dead.
>
> No grave was dug, no stone was laid,
> no bone was ever found,
> they say the flames took man and maid
> and left the empty ground.
>
> *So raise a cup to Treveth's dead...*

> [!quote] Boots at Treveth (the soldiers' song)
>
> A Queensman camped at Treveth, boys,
> a Freehand camped there too,
> at dawn there wasn't any noise,
> just empty boots, by two.
>
> *Oh, keep your boots on, lads, I say,*
> *and keep the fire bright,*
> *the sergeant counts the boots by day,*
> *not men who ran by night.*
>
> The captain sent a company,
> a hundred on the roll,
> at dawn, a hundred pair to see,
> and not a single soul.
>
> The Freehands swore the crown's men ran,
> the crown swore Freehands fled,
> but both left boots, a pair a man,
> in rows as by a bed.
>
> *Oh, keep your boots on, lads, I say...*
>
> So if they send you Treveth way,
> don't take your boots off there,
> for no one's come back, to this day,
> to fetch the other pair.
