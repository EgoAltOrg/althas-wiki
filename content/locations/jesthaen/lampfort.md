**Summary**: An old fort a day and a half west of [[drinmery|Drinmery]] along the river, where Saint Trefan held off the Mad King's host in [[valerions-heresy|Valerion's Heresy]].

---

## History

During [[valerions-heresy|Valerion's Heresy]], Saint Trefan held the fort for [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]] against the Mad King's host with only a handful of men. Each night they lit a lamp in every window and carried them from room to room, so that the crown's scouts counted a garrison of hundreds. The host dug in for a siege instead of storming the walls, and that bought enough time for the See's reinforcements to arrive. The fort has been called the Lampfort ever since. After the war, Trefan built the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]] in [[drinmery|Drinmery]], which bears his name.

Long after, the fort stood abandoned, and a gang of bandits made it their lair and raided Drinmery's market. At dawn on Adventus 15, 366 VR, the party attacked and destroyed the gang. [[novak-azimuth|Novak]] killed its leader and [[wick|Wick]] died in the fight. Of three bandits who surrendered, two were executed and [[jory-fenn|Jory Fenn]] was spared.
