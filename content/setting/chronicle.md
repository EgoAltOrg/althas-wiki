**Summary**: The chronicle of Althas: a day-by-day record of what has happened, kept as the days pass and filled in as older truths come to light.

---

This is the chronicle of Althas: a record of the days as they pass and of what each one held. Its days are reckoned by [[calendar|the calendar of Althas]] in the Voldaen Reckoning, and the record opens with the campaign now underway. Older events reach it too, set down under their own dates as they come to light, so a day can be written into the chronicle long after it has passed. For the wider sweep of history behind these days, see the [[timeline|Timeline]].

> [!note] How this chronicle is kept
> Each day that leaves a mark is given its own entry under its date, oldest first, grouped under the arc it belongs to, and days on which nothing of note happened are left unwritten. What stands here is only what has come to be known, so the record of the past grows alongside the present.

## Dead Men in Drinmery

### Adventus 12, 366 VR

The Inquisitor [[uriel-kenan|Uriel Kenan]] and the courier [[malak|Malak]] reach the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]] in [[drinmery|Drinmery]], where [[rosestripe|Rosestripe]] and Father [[ambrose-mourne|Tobias]] greet them and Uriel takes the Sinner [[rastaban|Rastaban]] into his custody from the Inquisitor [[gideon|Gideon]]. [[novak-azimuth|Novak Azimuth]], [[zalmir-aldarson|Zalmir Aldarson]] and [[leonard-moore|Leonard Moore]] enter the town by the north gate. Bandits raid the [[drinmery-market|market]]; they beat the guardsman [[pell|Pell]] and steal [[cornelia-azimuth|Cornelia Azimuth's]] ring from Leonard, and [[immanuel-greene|Immanuel Greene]] arrives with his men after the fight. That night, beneath the convent, the party opens the sealed tomb of Saint [[trefan|Trefan]] and finds the saint's body desecrated. In the churchyard above, Gideon digs up an unmarked grave and finds a body with a locket bearing the initials TW, and Malak wakes on the church step to see it. The party dines and sleeps at [[fleming-manor|Fleming Manor]] as the Count's guests, meets [[dorothea|Dorothea]], and Malak is paid with the deed to a decommissioned merchant ship.

### Adventus 13, 366 VR

Father [[ambrose-mourne|Tobias]] tells [[rosestripe|Rosestripe]] and [[novak-azimuth|Novak]] that he will leave Drinmery for [[andaluria|Andaluria]] to right his past wrongs, and shows Novak the [[the-threnodies|Threnody]] [[malak|Malak]] delivered. [[gideon|Gideon]] and [[uriel-kenan|Uriel]] agree to work together against the bandits and Father Tobias. [[leonard-moore|Leonard Moore]] leaves the party for Fleming Manor. At the [[restful-shade|Restful Shade]], [[alys|Alys]] tells the party how Father Tobias passed on her son Colm's last words, and Dorothea says her goodbye. In the afternoon the party sets out for the bandits' fort, a day and a half west along the river, with [[zalmir-aldarson|Zalmir]] as their guide.

### Adventus 15, 366 VR

At dawn the party reaches the bandits' fort and destroys the gang. Novak kills its leader and [[wick|Wick]] dies in the fight. Of three bandits who surrender, two are executed, and one is spared to testify against [[immanuel-greene|Immanuel Greene]].

### Adventus 16, 366 VR

Outside Drinmery, Gideon gives Uriel his sword and leaves the party, asking them to tell Father Tobias he died fighting the bandits. In the evening the party finds [[leonard-moore|Leonard Moore]] hanged naked in the town square, a sign reading "Death to Queensmen" around his neck and [[cornelia-azimuth|Cornelia Azimuth's]] ring on his hand.

That night the party takes the ring from Leonard's body, and Father Tobias gives [[novak-azimuth|Novak]] Leonard's last words. [[immanuel-greene|Greene]] and [[gideon|Gideon]] lead Greene's men up the convent hill, and at the churchyard gate Gideon unmasks the priest as Ambrose Mourne of [[house-mourne|House Mourne]], an excommunicated heretic who murdered the true Tobias Wren. Ambrose raises the churchyard dead. Greene dies, and Ambrose dies to a wolf spirit and [[rosestripe|Rosestripe]]; of Greene's company only [[pell|Pell]] and the smith [[garrick-thorne|Garrick Thorne]] survive.

### Adventus 17, 366 VR

Gideon leaves for the [[the-holy-see|Holy See]] with Ambrose's head. The party rests at the tavern. Pell becomes Novak's squire, and [[yselda-marrow|Yselda Marrow]] reveals herself as a Syndic of the [[guild|Guild]].

### Adventus 18, 366 VR

The party leaves Drinmery for [[jestha|Jestha]] with Pell, Yselda and [[jory-fenn|Jory Fenn]].

## Treveth Arc

### Adventus 25, 366 VR

A week down the road, at the crossroads where the road to the border towns parts from the old road to Jestha, the party meets [[d-the-bard|D, the Bard]]. They search the empty village of [[treveth|Treveth]] and meet [[caz-the-wanderer|Caz, the Wanderer]] at the door of [[fustierhall|Fustierhall]]. Driven by a face in the fog, they enter the manor. That same day in [[drinmery|Drinmery]], [[greed|Greed]] kills the gravekeeper [[aldous-pike|Aldous Pike]] in the convent churchyard and takes the headless body of [[ambrose-mourne|Ambrose Mourne]], which walks again.
