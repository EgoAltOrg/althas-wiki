**Summary**: The chronicle of Althas: a day-by-day record of what has happened, kept as the days pass and filled in as older truths come to light.

---

This is the chronicle of Althas: a record of the days as they pass and of what each one held. Its days are reckoned by [[calendar|the calendar of Althas]] in the Voldaen Reckoning, and the record opens with the campaign now underway. Older events reach it too, set down under their own dates as they come to light, so a day can be written into the chronicle long after it has passed. For the wider sweep of history behind these days, see the [[timeline|Timeline]].

> [!note] How this chronicle is kept
> Each day that leaves a mark is given its own entry under its date, oldest first, and days on which nothing of note happened are left unwritten. What stands here is only what has come to be known, so the record of the past grows alongside the present.

## Day 12 of Adventus, 366 VR

The Inquisitor [[uriel-kenan|Uriel Kenan]] and the courier [[malak|Malak]] reach the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]] in [[drinmery|Drinmery]], where [[rosestripe|Rosestripe]] and [[tobias-wren|Father Tobias]] greet them and Uriel takes the Sinner [[rastaban|Rastaban]] into his custody from the Inquisitor [[gideon|Gideon]]. [[novak-azimuth|Novak Azimuth]], [[zalmir-aldarson|Zalmir Aldarson]] and [[leonard-moore|Leonard Moore]] enter the town by the north gate. Bandits raid the market; they beat the guardsman [[pell|Pell]] and steal [[cornelia-azimuth|Cornelia Azimuth's]] ring from Leonard, and [[immanuel-greene|Immanuel Greene]] arrives with his men after the fight. That night, beneath the convent, the party opens the sealed tomb of Saint Trefan and finds the saint's body desecrated.
