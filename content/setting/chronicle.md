**Summary**: The chronicle of Althas: a day-by-day record of what has happened, kept as the days pass and filled in as older truths come to light.

---

This is the chronicle of Althas: a record of the days as they pass and of what each one held. Its days are reckoned by [[calendar|the calendar of Althas]] in the Voldaen Reckoning, and the record opens with the campaign now underway. Older events reach it too, set down under their own dates as they come to light, so a day can be written into the chronicle long after it has passed. For the wider sweep of history behind these days, see the [[timeline|Timeline]].

> [!note] How this chronicle is kept
> Each day that leaves a mark is given its own entry under its date, oldest first, and days on which nothing of note happened are left unwritten. What stands here is only what has come to be known, so the record of the past grows alongside the present.

## Adventus 12, 366 VR

The Inquisitor [[uriel-kenan|Uriel Kenan]] and the courier [[malak|Malak]] reach the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]] in [[drinmery|Drinmery]], where [[rosestripe|Rosestripe]] and [[tobias-wren|Father Tobias]] greet them and Uriel takes the Sinner [[rastaban|Rastaban]] into his custody from the Inquisitor [[gideon|Gideon]]. [[novak-azimuth|Novak Azimuth]], [[zalmir-aldarson|Zalmir Aldarson]] and [[leonard-moore|Leonard Moore]] enter the town by the north gate. Bandits raid the [[drinmery-market|market]]; they beat the guardsman [[pell|Pell]] and steal [[cornelia-azimuth|Cornelia Azimuth's]] ring from Leonard, and [[immanuel-greene|Immanuel Greene]] arrives with his men after the fight. That night, beneath the convent, the party opens the sealed tomb of Saint Trefan and finds the saint's body desecrated. In the churchyard above, Gideon digs up an unmarked grave and finds a body with a locket bearing the initials TW, and Malak wakes on the church step to see it. The party dines and sleeps at [[fleming-manor|Fleming Manor]] as the Count's guests, meets [[dorothea|Dorothea]], and Malak is paid with the deed to a decommissioned merchant ship.

## Adventus 13, 366 VR

[[tobias-wren|Father Tobias]] tells [[rosestripe|Rosestripe]] and [[novak-azimuth|Novak]] that he will leave Drinmery for [[andaluria|Andaluria]] to right his past wrongs, and shows Novak the [[the-threnodies|Threnody]] [[malak|Malak]] delivered. [[gideon|Gideon]] and [[uriel-kenan|Uriel]] agree to work together against the bandits and Father Tobias. [[leonard-moore|Leonard Moore]] leaves the party for Fleming Manor. At the [[restful-shade|Restful Shade]], [[alys|Alys]] tells the party how Father Tobias passed on her son Colm's last words, and Dorothea says her goodbye. In the afternoon the party sets out for the bandits' fort, a day and a half west along the river, with [[zalmir-aldarson|Zalmir]] as their guide.

## Adventus 15, 366 VR

At dawn the party reaches the bandits' fort and destroys the gang. Novak kills its leader and [[wick|Wick]] dies in the fight. Of three bandits who surrender, two are executed, and one is spared to testify against [[immanuel-greene|Immanuel Greene]].

## Adventus 16, 366 VR

Outside Drinmery, Gideon gives Uriel his sword and leaves the party, asking them to tell Father Tobias he died fighting the bandits. In the evening the party finds [[leonard-moore|Leonard Moore]] hanged naked in the town square, a sign reading "Death to Queensmen" around his neck and [[cornelia-azimuth|Cornelia Azimuth's]] ring on his hand.
