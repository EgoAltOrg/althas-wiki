**Summary**: The Freehands, the army [[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]] rallied from the scattered cells of Voldaen's south to win [[jesthaen|Jesthaen]]'s revolution. Today the name belongs to the extremists who refused the peace and want no government at all.

---

## Overview

"Free hand" was a field word before it was a name. On the debt-bound estates of southern [[voldaen|Voldaen]] it meant a laborer who had carried their own way out. In the revolution it became the name of the army that broke the south away and founded the Republic. Today it belongs to the anarchists who say the Republic betrayed that army, and who have never been rooted out of [[jestha|Jestha]].

## Structure & Governance

The Freehands have no leaders and keep no rolls. They work in cells, small groups who know one another and little else. Some cells only steal and smuggle; others kill. A cell answers to no one, and when several act together it is because each chose to.

## Beliefs & Purpose

Freehands hold that people are free only where there are no laws and no masters. In their telling, the revolution was meant to tear the crown's state and [[the-holy-see|the Church]] down to their foundations. Instead its heroes took the old order's seats: [[aldric-voldis|Aldric]] the head of the Republic, and [[ezekiel-ashmark|Ezekiel Ashmark]] the Archbishop's chair. To the Freehands, Aldric is a false prophet, a royal bastard who used their beliefs to start a revolution and put himself and his allies back in power. Many of them want the war with Voldaen back, because the war was the only time the fields were free.

## History

### The cells

Before the revolution, resistance on Voldaen's debt-bound estates, above all the southern plantations, grew as many separate cells. A road passed runaways from hand to hand through barns, chapels and kitchens, each stop knowing only the next. Harvest congresses, where the plantations sent delegates, met disguised as feasts. In the towns, thieves' crews preyed on the nobility, some only robbing them, others assassinating them.

### The army

[[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]] came south from [[the-witherwatch|the Witherwatch]] and rallied the cells into one army, the Freehands. With [[ezekiel-ashmark|Ezekiel Ashmark]] and his other allies he led it through the revolution of 351 VR and the ten years of war that followed. The congresses joined on a promise that every jailed field hand would go free, and fed the army by running the harvest past the landlords' collectors. The road carried its letters, soldiers and arms, the beginning of the smuggling routes recorded on [[diplomacy|Diplomacy]].

### The peace

When the Jesthaen Treaty ended the fighting in 361 VR, the new nation needed a government. The new Republic kept or reformed the institutions it needed to govern instead of tearing them down, so that a stable state could protect it. The Senate, each Senator elected by a region of Jesthaen, elected Aldric to lead it. Most of the army accepted the compromise. Those who refused it kept the name.

## Notable Members

- [[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]] and [[ezekiel-ashmark|Ezekiel Ashmark]] led the Freehands army in the revolution. The Freehands of today count them as traitors.
- [[bram-tolliver|Bram Tolliver]], once of a town crew, now Marshal of Jestha, whom they count among the worst of the traitors.
