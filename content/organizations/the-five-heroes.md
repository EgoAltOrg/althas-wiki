**Summary**: The five heroes who fought and brought down [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] at [[hilltop|Hilltop]] in 333-334 VR, the event that reshaped Althas's politics and its faith: the Voldaen High Prince [[edrion-voldis|Edrion Voldis]], [[cassio-aquila|Cassio Aquila]] of [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]], [[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]], [[lael|Lael the Reborn]], and [[lyra-aquila|Lyra Aquila]] of [[polaris|Polaris]].

---

## History

### The battle

In 333 VR the [[the-ophanim|Ophanim]] landed at [[hilltop|Hilltop]] and devastated it, forcing [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]] to relocate to central Althas. The following year, 334 VR, the Five Heroes brought the Ophanim down. Its death shook the faith and Althas itself to their roots. Of late, the nights over the land around its corpse have begun to lengthen without end.

## Notable Members

### The heroes

- **[[edrion-voldis|Edrion Voldis]]**, High Prince of [[voldaen|Voldaen]] and father of Queen [[valis-voldis|Valis Voldis]], died in the fighting, setting off the Voldis Succession Crisis.
- **[[cassio-aquila|Cassio Aquila]]** of [[the-holy-see|the Holy See]] and [[house-aquila|House Aquila]], a scholar of the age and bearer of the [[miracles|Miracle of Pentecost]], also died bringing the Ophanim down.
- **[[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]]**, baseborn half-brother of Edrion, survived: honored by the soldiery and the smallfolk but spurned by the capital's oldest houses, he became the champion of the common people and founded the [[jesthaen|Jesthaen Republic]].
- **[[lael|Lael]], called the Reborn**, fell in the fighting and was returned to life by his [[miracles|Miracle of Rebirth]], which keeps his body from ever truly dying. He is remembered as the hero who could not stay dead.
- **[[lyra-aquila|Lyra Aquila]]** of [[polaris|Polaris]] and [[house-aquila|House Aquila]], twin sister to [[cassio-aquila|Cassio Aquila]] and a member of the Triumvirate of Polaris, survived and set the whole account down in her book *Verba Ophanim*, from the Ophanim's arrival at Hilltop to its dying words, a work only House Aquila's Miracles of Pentecost and Anamnesis could have produced. She is seldom seen now, a reclusive Archmage who rarely descends from her tower; whether grief, her [[the-ophanim|OphDreams]], or only her work keeps her there, rumor cannot agree.
