## Overview

The Triumvirate is the government of [[polaris|Polaris]]: three Archmages who rule the magocracy together, each holding a seat by having won it. It was born with the nation, out of [[the-lodestar-pact|the Lodestar Pact]] of 167 VR. Today [[lyra-aquila|Lyra Aquila]] and [[antares-algol|Antares Algol]] hold two of the seats. The third, [[hesper-arcturus|Hesper Arcturus]]'s, has stood empty since her death in 363 VR, and with Lyra seldom leaving her tower, Antares is in practice the one who governs.

## Structure & Governance

### The challenge

A seat on the Triumvirate is won by challenge: a mage of Polaris challenges a sitting Archmage, and the duel decides the seat. The law asks one thing of a challenge, that it be witnessed. A duel no one saw is no challenge, and a mage killed in one was murdered.

The loser's fate is the victor's to decide. The law sets no terms for yielding and none for mercy, and by Polaris custom the beaten mage dies.

### The empty seat

When a seat falls empty, the sitting Archmages convene a trial for it, a tournament of mages fought under the same law. In the three years since Hesper's death, no trial has been called.

## Beliefs & Purpose

Polaris holds that might makes right. Status in the magocracy follows arcane aptitude, and the Triumvirate is its sharpest form: the strongest mages rule, and the weak are culled. Mercy in a challenge is rare enough that Polaris remembers who showed it.

## History

### Founding

The Triumvirate was created in 167 VR by [[the-lodestar-pact|the Lodestar Pact]], the negotiated partition by which the scholars and mages of the north broke from [[voldaen|Voldaen]] and founded [[polaris|Polaris]] as an independent magocracy.

### Hesper and Izar

For years Hesper's own son, [[izar-arcturus|Izar]], challenged her for her seat and lost every time. She always refused to kill him. Polaris read the ritual as something close to a mercy Izar extended an "old stubborn woman", giving her something to win at.

In 363 VR Hesper was killed by a [[giants|Giant]] within her own walls, during her final duel with Izar, and the news plunged Polaris into the Errai Riots. Her seat has stood empty since.

## Notable Members

- [[lyra-aquila|Lyra Aquila]], of [[house-aquila|House Aquila]], one of [[the-five-heroes|the Five Heroes]] and author of *Verba Ophanim*; seldom seen outside her tower.
- [[antares-algol|Antares Algol]], of House Algol, bearer of the [[miracles|Miracle of Abrogation]]; in practice the power that governs Polaris.
- [[hesper-arcturus|Hesper Arcturus]], of House Arcturus, killed in 363 VR. House Arcturus's [[miracles|Miracle of Firmament]] has produced a long line of Archmages.
