**Summary**: **The Witherwatch**, the standing garrison [[voldaen|Voldaen]] keeps at the [[godless-gate|Godless Gate]], holding the blighted [[witherwild-continent|Witherwild]] frontier back from Althas since the fall of [[haven|Haven]]. A grim, thankless posting, and the wall between Althas and what the Folly left behind.

---

## Overview

When [[haven|Haven]] fell and the Witherwild's growth and beasts began to press through the [[godless-gate|Godless Gate]] toward Althas, [[voldaen|Voldaen]] raised the Witherwatch to hold the pass. It has held it ever since, generations of watchmen bleeding to keep the blight on its own side of the mountains. The posting carries little glory and less reward: its ranks are filled with condemned men given the Watch in place of a noose, and with inconvenient sons and rival claimants a great house would rather see buried at the edge of the world than left standing near a throne. Its most storied commander was one such exile: [[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]], the baseborn prince sent north to lead the Watch and be rid of, who abandoned his post and marched south to found the [[jesthaen|Jesthaen Republic]]. Althas at large gives the frontier and its guardians scarcely a thought. Yet the Watch is the whole reason the Folly's ruin has stayed the Witherwild's problem and not the continent's.
