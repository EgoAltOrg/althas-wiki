## Overview

House Fleming holds [[drinmery|Drinmery]]. It owns the city's palaces, founded its courts and taxes its trade, which makes it the leading house in this stretch of [[jesthaen|Jesthaen]]. Its head is Count [[guilmore-fleming|Guilmore Fleming]], who sits as the city's Senator and is still called by the title he held before the revolution. The family's seat in the city is [[fleming-manor|Fleming Manor]].
