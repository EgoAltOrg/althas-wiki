**Summary**: An ancient [[voldaen|Voldaen]] Great House, bearer of the [[miracles|Miracle of Fames]] and keeper of the Famesfeast, the cannibal rite behind its Miracle. Once Ministers of Domestic Affairs, the Olnir were stripped of that office after [[lorkhan-olnir|Lorkhan Olnir]] sided with [[aldric-voldis|Aldric]]'s rebellion, and hold their noble standing now only by the fealty of a newly raised head, Maxim Olnir.

---

## Overview

House Olnir is one of the oldest and most privileged of [[voldaen|Voldaen's]] Great Houses, its line reaching back to the Divine Age's war against [[the-ones-below|the Ones Below]]. Fighting alongside [[the-one-above|the One Above]], the House's founder, Soren, was granted the [[miracles|Miracle of Fames]] and holyblood, both blessings passed to every generation since.

### The Miracle of Fames

The [[miracles|Miracle of Fames]] lets its bearer take the might of the fallen into himself, the strength or faculty gained matched to what is claimed from the dead. A scholar's mind may sharpen the wits; a warrior's heart may harden the body. Each of the fallen yields but a single such boon, the claiming exacts a heavy toll of body and mind, and the fresher the dead, the greater the gain. An Olnir claims his due by eating a chosen organ of the fallen.

## Beliefs & Purpose

### The Famesfeast

Central to the House is the Famesfeast, a weekly rite preceded by a day-long fast, which every Olnir child is made to attend from the youngest age. Though Fames blesses at most a single child in a generation, all must partake, for the rite hardens and binds the House's young as surely as it serves the gift. It is a cannibal rite: the House feasts on human flesh, and Fames itself is fed by eating a chosen organ of the fallen. The Olnir do little to hide it. Outsiders know the House instead by its creed, resourcefulness, ruthlessness, the rule of might, and the survival of the fittest above all, and by the quiet dread the name Olnir carries.

## History

### Standing and history

That same creed likely guided the Olnir when they sided with [[valeran-voldis|Valeran Voldis]], later the Blessed King, against [[valerion-voldis|Valerion]], the Mad King, in the course of [[valerions-heresy|his great heresy]]. The allegiance earned them the status of Great House, and for generations thereafter the Olnir held the office of Minister of Domestic Affairs.

The office was revoked only recently, as punishment for [[lorkhan-olnir|Lorkhan Olnir]], the House's missing heir, who sided with [[aldric-voldis|Aldric Voldis]] during the revolution that founded the [[jesthaen|Jesthaen Republic]]. The Olnir kept their noble standing only after their new head, Maxim Olnir, swore fealty to the Voldaen Crown.

Their coat of arms bears a bull and a cross in deep red and black, beneath the motto *Vires Eorum Nostrae*, "their strength, ours." Their ancestral seat is the fortress of Mambaril, where Olnir children are raised to the Famesfeast.
