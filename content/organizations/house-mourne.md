## Overview

House Mourne was one of [[hilltop|Hilltop's]] pureblooded houses, kin in kind to House Cruoris though never as great. Where other houses gave the faith its scholars and confessors, Mourne gave it the voices of the dead.

## Beliefs & Purpose

### The office of the dead

The Mourne bore their signature [[miracles|Miracle]], the Miracle of Testament, which compels a soul to answer over its body. Their calling followed from it: they sat with the dying, carried the last words of the faithful home, and testified in [[the-holy-see|the Church's]] own tribunals, calling the murdered to name the hand that killed them. It was a small house, and a trusted one.

## History

### The Descent

House Mourne was all but destroyed when [[the-ophanim|the Ophanim]] fell on [[hilltop|Hilltop]] in 333 VR. Only [[ambrose-mourne|Ambrose Mourne]] came through it, the last of the blood and the last bearer of Testament. The Church later excommunicated him for heresy.

## Notable Members

- [[ambrose-mourne|Ambrose Mourne]]: the last of the house. He lived nearly thirty years as Father Tobias Wren of [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Saint Trefan]], and died in its churchyard on the night of Adventus 16, 366 VR.
