## History

Mother Wenna keeps the Restful Shade, the little-known brothel associated with the [[wakeful-lamp|Wakeful Lamp]] in [[drinmery|Drinmery]]. She told [[uriel-kenan|Uriel]] that the bandits never attack General [[immanuel-greene|Greene's]] carriages, only those of other merchants.
