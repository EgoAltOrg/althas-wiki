## History

Alys is a townswoman of [[drinmery|Drinmery]] whose young son, Colm, died shortly before the party arrived. She comes to the church of the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]] to grieve for him.

The party found her at the [[restful-shade|Restful Shade]], where she works. She told them that at Colm's funeral, [[tobias-wren|Father Tobias]] put his ear to the boy's mouth and passed his last words on to her.
