## Overview

Alys is an ordinary townswoman of [[drinmery|Drinmery]], recently bereaved: her young son, Colm, died not long before the party's arrival, and she grieves him without consolation. She comes to the church of the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]] to mourn him.
