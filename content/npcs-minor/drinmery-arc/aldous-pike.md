**Summary**: The old gravekeeper of the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan's]] churchyard in [[drinmery|Drinmery]].

---

## Appearance

A gaunt old man with lank dark hair under a battered hat, in a long buttoned coat, leaning on a crooked stick.

## History

Pike keeps the churchyard of the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]]. He told [[malak|Malak]] that [[alys|Alys]] comes to the church to grieve her son Colm, who died not long ago.
