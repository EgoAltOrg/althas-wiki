**Summary**: The old gravekeeper of the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan's]] churchyard in [[drinmery|Drinmery]], killed on Adventus 25, 366 VR.

---

## Appearance

A gaunt old man with lank dark hair under a battered hat, in a long buttoned coat, leaning on a crooked stick.

## History

Pike kept the churchyard of the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|Convent of Saint Trefan]]. He told [[malak|Malak]] that [[alys|Alys]] came to the church to grieve her son Colm, who died not long ago.

On the night of Adventus 16, 366 VR, he came down to the town square to tell the party that the priest would do the dying rites for [[leonard-moore|Leonard Moore]]. On Adventus 25, 366 VR, [[greed|Greed]] killed him in the churchyard, and his head burst. Greed then took the headless body of [[ambrose-mourne|Ambrose Mourne]].
