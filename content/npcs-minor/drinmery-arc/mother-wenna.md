## History

Mother Wenna keeps the Restful Shade, the little-known brothel associated with the Wakeful Lamp in [[drinmery|Drinmery]]. She told [[uriel-kenan|Uriel]] that the bandits never attack [[immanuel-greene|General Greene's]] carriages, only those of other merchants.
