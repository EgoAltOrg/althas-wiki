**Summary**: The barracks smith of [[drinmery|Drinmery]], one of [[immanuel-greene|Greene's]] company, now held in the town's jail.

---

## History

Garrick Thorne was one of Greene's men in the battle at the [[convent-of-saint-trefan|convent]] on the night of Adventus 16, 366 VR. With [[pell|Pell]], he was the only one of the company to survive it. Afterward he was jailed in Drinmery.
